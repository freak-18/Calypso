## 0. BRAND FOUNDATION (for reference — use this to guide tone across every page)

**Name:** Calypso Website Builders
**Tagline:** "We Build Your Digital Presence."
**Alt taglines:** "Your Business, Online and Unstoppable." / "Websites That Work as Hard as You Do."

**Positioning:**
Calypso is not a cheap freelancer — it's a digital partner. We help businesses turn their brand into a professional, revenue-generating online presence. Every project is priced on business value (leads, credibility, sales) not on hours typed.

**Mission:** To give growing businesses a digital presence that looks and performs like it belongs to a much bigger company.

**Vision:** To become the go-to website partner for small and mid-sized businesses who are ready to be taken seriously online.

**Target customers:**
- Local businesses without a website (or with an outdated one)
- Startups and small teams launching their first digital presence
- Service businesses (elevators, consultancies, clinics, agencies) needing credibility
- E-commerce entrepreneurs ready to sell online

**Unique Selling Proposition:**
Calypso combines technical execution with brand strategy — you don't just get a website, you get a digital presence built to convert visitors into clients.

**Brand personality:** Modern • Trustworthy • Creative • Technical

**Visual identity direction:**
- **Primary colors:** Deep Ocean Navy (#0B1D3A) + Calypso Teal (#00C2B2)
- **Secondary/accent:** Coral Orange (#FF6B4A) for CTAs, Soft White (#F7F9FA) for backgrounds
- **Typography:** Headings — "Sora" or "Space Grotesk" (modern, technical); Body — "Inter" (clean, readable)
- **Icon style:** Line icons, rounded corners, minimal — consistent 2px stroke
- **Logo direction:** Wordmark "CALYPSO" in bold geometric sans-serif, with a small wave/pulse mark above the "Y" symbolizing digital connectivity + the sea. Secondary lockup: icon-only mark for favicon/social.
- **Social identity:** Dark navy backgrounds, teal/coral accents, consistent line-icon system, before/after website mockup posts.
